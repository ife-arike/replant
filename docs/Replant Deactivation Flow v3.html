1Card too small + centered in dead space. Padding values were tuned for the 280-px wireframe and stayed the same in production — on a 430-wide screen the card reads as a small island floating mid-viewport.
2Body text is center-aligned. On long copy this creates orphan words ("us.") on their own line and ragged middle-aligned paragraphs. Looks unintentional, reads worse than left-aligned.
3Backdrop is flat & opaque (rgba(8,8,8,0.88)). The login behind disappears entirely, and the card surface (#0F0F0F) sits on a near-identical tone — no depth, no sense of "modal over screen."
4Email still reads connect@. Per the late-stage spec change it should be [email protected].
