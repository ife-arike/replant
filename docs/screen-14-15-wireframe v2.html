"What do you need?" — layout decision

Chosen: 2×2 grid (CSS Grid 1fr 1fr, gap 10).

Reasoning: chips at 430 pt and free-wrap produced an orphan "Just to be heard" on row 2. The full-width toggle-row alternative reads too much like a settings list and dilutes the chip metaphor. The grid keeps all four cells equal, preserves chip pill shape, breaks the wrap, and bumps each cell to 44 pt for hit-target compliance. Copy is locked per brief — no shortening of "Practical support" or "Just to be heard."
