A young mother of twenty-two, newly baptised, imprisoned with her infant son at her breast. Her father begged her to renounce; she pointed to a water jug and asked whether it could be called by any other name than what it was. “Neither can I call myself anything other than what I am, a Christian.” She recorded her own prison visions in her own hand, the earliest surviving writing we have from a Christian woman, and walked into the arena singing.

Red on this screen
Red means someone is in danger.
Urgent on Prayer Wall, persecuted here — one sentence true on both tabs, so a leader never re-learns it. Five marks carry it. Red never touches chrome again: no title, no card borders, no section rules, no spinners, no eyebrows.
The filled dot. Critical or Urgent — the top two tiers. On Prayer Wall this same mark is the urgent request.
The hollow ring. Serious, Ongoing, Informational. Still red, because every heartcry is an escalated request already — sky read as routine, and nothing here is routine. Hollow is weight without immediacy.
A The tier. The sender's own word — Critical · Urgent · Serious · Ongoing · Informational — red for the top two, brightness below. Only Critical pulses, 2600ms, the one thing worth animating. Five pulsing labels would be the old flood.
The margin rule. 1.5px beside the preview of an active row — inherited verbatim from Prayer Wall so an urgent row and an active row have identical anatomy. It is what makes the row findable mid-scroll without hue doing the work alone.
The seal. One place only — a martyr, on Witness of the day. Red as past tense.
What changed
4 pills + 8 region chips → 3 tabs, gliding indicator, country behind the filter mark.
My Heartcries → My Voice in the header, where Journal sits. Everyone in the feed is “a voice”; yours is your own.
The unread notice replaces the whole NotifBar — one sky dot beside My Voice.
Feed cards → hairline rows. No fill, no borders, no red left rules.
The “Are you suffering” card is back as it was — surface fill, centred, full-width button, red label. The one interactive red in the app, on the one action taken from inside danger.
The security promise moved off the header and into that card, where it is load-bearing.
Witnesses runs living → Witness of the day → Around the world. Stories lead to the blog, not to per-article links.
The filter readout carries the section name: “Heartcries from the body”, or from one country.
Green is gone. Received · Seen · Responded reads by brightness; “Open secure message” is a sky text action.
“Active” is gone. It was never a tier — SeverityTag renders the DB's critical as the label “Active”, so the feed showed a state where the sender had chosen a tier. The card now shows the tier, identical on the feed and in My Voice.
“Tap to open” came off the eyebrow row — the tier occupies that slot on every card now.
Guidance icons became a quiet 01–04 index rather than four drawn glyphs.
