Link Format — Founder still to rule
Two options surfaced in the UI. Option A · projectreplant.org/join?ref=… is the CD recommendation: works on any device, in any browser, in SMS, in email, with or without the Replant app installed — the only format that actually behaves at the invite stage. Option B · replant://join?ref=… only opens if the recipient already has the app, which defeats the purpose of an invite. Whichever format is selected becomes the share payload; the unselected one is hidden post-decision and this whole selector collapses.

Section CTA destinations — live in Tweaks panel
The "Where the Body bears" type chips and the "Across the network" feed items both route through the Outreach destination tweak (Editorial · Prayer Wall · DM · ComingSoon). The "Outreach near you" feed items always route to navigate('OutreachDetail', { needId }) — a per-need page with location, supplies list, contact, and a "Stand with this need" CTA. The "How to help" rows are fixed routes (Pray / Give / Go) and not tweakable.
Live data — outreach_needs + outreach_areas
outreach_needs (feed items): id, area_key (FK), title, meta, city, distance_km (nullable for restricted), restricted (bool), contact_user_id, active. Geo-resolved against the leader's verified ministry location for the Near you section. Restricted items (encrypted region) hide distance + city, surface a "Location withheld" chip, and require verified-leader access.

outreach_areas (type chips): key, numeral, name, scripture_ref, route. Seeded with the six chips shown above. The Scripture ref is part of the area — non-negotiable, kept in DB so it can't be forgotten in copy.
Strict — no money facilitation
The "Give" action is surplus matching, not giving money. Per FAQ Q12, Replant does not facilitate direct financial transfers. Copy on this screen, the linked screens, and the network bear note at bottom must reinforce that. "Give of your surplus" is the canonical label; do not abbreviate to "Give" alone in routing copy that could be mistaken for monetary giving.
Why no per-category colour
Status colours (red / amber / green) belong to thresholds in this design system — verification states, alerts, urgency. Using them as decoration on editorial chips collapses that semantic meaning. Monochrome holds the sacred register and keeps the colour palette load-bearing.

Content — verbatim, locked (13 questions)
01 · What is Replant? Replant is a secure global network that connects Christian leaders — clergy, elders, and ministry leaders — across churches, cities, and continents for prayer, support, and unity.

02 · Who is this for? Replant is for Christian leaders across all church expressions. The only requirement is a sincere confession of Jesus Christ as Lord and Saviour.

03 · Can a ministry secretary or church administrator join on behalf of the leader? Replant is built so that ministry leaders can have a direct line to what's happening in the church and with one another — leader to leader. We'd really love for the leader themselves to register and join, not because we don't value your role, but because we want them to have that direct connection from day one. If they're available, we'd encourage them to complete this registration — you're always welcome to walk through it together with them.

04 · Is this a social media platform? No. Replant is not a social media platform, and that is intentional. There are no likes, no follower counts, no algorithms deciding what you see, and no ministry promotion.

05 · What is the foundation of Replant? The Holy Bible is the only source of truth on this platform. No exceptions.

06 · Can house churches and underground churches join? Absolutely. House churches, churches without walls, online congregations, and underground churches are not only eligible — they are actively encouraged to join.

07 · Can a pastor remain anonymous on the network? Yes — a pastor may remain anonymous within the network. Their personal name can be hidden from other leaders. Their church or ministry name, however, is always visible. Real identity information is always required at registration for security and legal purposes, and is kept strictly confidential.

08 · Is there a limit per church? A maximum of two (2) pastors or leaders may register per church or ministry on the Replant network.

09 · What if two churches share a name? This is acceptable. Location information is used to distinguish churches that share the same name.

10 · Are conversations private? Conversations within Replant are protected within the network and are not shared externally.

11 · How is the Persecuted Church section protected? Submissions are encrypted. Sensitive details are accessible only to verified leaders within the network. Underground church locations are never made public.

12 · Can we send financial support through Replant? At this time, Replant does not facilitate direct financial transfers between ministries.

13 · How can churches actually help one another right now? Through prayer, resources, manpower, advice, and encouragement.
