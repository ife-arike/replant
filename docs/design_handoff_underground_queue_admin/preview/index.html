The verification queue — two sets of eyes on every life-safety ruling

Fifteen desktop surfaces extending admin.projectreplant.org/underground from a read-only viewer into a working queue. One or two trained admins move underground churches from Pending to Verified or Deactivated — never alone. Every verify, reject, code rotation, and visibility change is a two-eyes proposal: Admin A proposes, Admin B confirms. The hero of this pass is the SLA band system — five day-count states the founder must ratify, especially the yellow-vs-amber distinction at days 15.

Father, we lift up the persecuted Church — believers gathering under watched doors in Iran, China, Eritrea, North Korea, and every nation where Your name is met with prison. We pray for the underground leaders whose verifications pass through these screens; let the waiting never crush them. Steady the one or two admins who make these life-safety decisions — give them sober minds, two sets of eyes, and Your wisdom. Let every pixel here guard Your people rather than expose them. In Jesus' name, Amen.

04 · Deliverable 4 · #1 visual ratification ask

The five SLA bands, side by side

A church has a 30-day window with checkpoints at days 5, 15, and 25. The day-count pill on every Pending row carries the band. The founder's explicit ask: see yellow (days 5–14) and amber (days 15–24) rendered next to each other and confirm they read as visibly distinct. Yellow is the "decision-or-info is due" nudge; amber is the "final-by-25 is approaching" warning. They are different colors, different weights, different urgency.

Days 0–4 Day 2·contact within day 5 Neutral · gray Fresh. First contact is owed by day 5. No pressure yet — the admin is inside the safe window. checkpoint · contact_by = 5
Days 5–14 Day 8·decision-or-info by day 15 Yellow Contact window has opened. A decision or a request for info is due by day 15. The gentle deadline. checkpoint · decide_by = 15
Days 15–24 Day 17·final by day 25 Amber Past the day-15 checkpoint. A final ruling is owed by day 25. Warmer, heavier — the warning state. checkpoint · final_by = 25
Days 25–29 Day 26·final overdue Red Final ruling overdue. At day 25 the row auto-routes to the Founder as sole owner (Ruling #13). routed → founder
Day 30+ Day 31·past window — auto-reject pending Pulsing red Past the 30-day window. Day-30 auto-reject fires for an unresponsive leader. The dot pulses. auto_reject · unresponsive
Ratify
The yellow→amber step happens at day 15. Yellow says "decide or ask"; amber says "final is coming." A separate stalled_pending sub-state pauses the clock when the leader is responsive but the admin is overloaded — the leader is never auto-rejected for the team's backlog (Ruling #6).
FE note One <SlaPill day={n} /> component derives band from days_since_submitted against the 5/15/25/30 thresholds. stalled_pending freezes the clock and renders the neutral-gray treatment with a paused glyph. Pulsing is gated behind prefers-reduced-motion.

09–10 · Deliverables 9 & 10

Admin B confirms — or counters, without rejecting the church

Admin B sees A's proposal verbatim — A's notes, A's evidence tier, and the exact leader-facing text that will be shown if confirmed — with a live 72h TTL countdown. B can Confirm, or Decline with required counter-notes (visible to A only), which returns the row to Untouched and notifies A. A decline is not a rejection of the church.

11–12 · Deliverables 11 & 12

Visibility override & the join-code machine — guarded by a spoken token

Changing a church between Visible and Hidden requires a 4-digit relay token — the code the leader spoke aloud during the T2 verification call. The admin types what they heard; a mismatch blocks the change. Anti-social-engineering. The join-code panel is a small state machine: hashed indicator → Rotate (a two-eyes proposal, action=rotate_join_code) → one-time re-reveal of the freshly minted code.

Live Open the override modal and type into the 4-digit token cells. The relay-token style is tweakable.

13–15 · Deliverables 13, 14 & 15

The Deactivated tab, the typed delete, and the locked door

Deactivated rows show the deactivation date/type, a days-until-hard-delete countdown, the last-touching admin, a single-admin Reinstate, and Hard-delete. Hard-delete demands the admin type the church_code to confirm — strictly one church at a time, no bulk select. And if an admin without the flag lands here, the surface refuses them gently.
