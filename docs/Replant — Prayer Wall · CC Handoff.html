KAN-PW-A ~3 pts

Multi-select filter bar — Urgent stack + category chips

Ready

Replace the single-select pill row with a multi-select bar. Two semantic groups on one row: Urgent (tone modifier, red) on the left of a hairline divider; category chips (sky) on the right. Both groups multi-select independently. Bar promotes to a 2-row stack at 3+ active categories. Clear chip pins right edge once any chip is active.

Frames
01·A idle01·B one-cat01·C Urgent+cat01·D 3+ stack01·E empty
Deliverables
  • Pill chip component with idle / selected / Urgent-idle / Urgent-selected states
  • Sticky filter row at top of feed (under top bar)
  • Active-count strip (renders only when ≥1 chip active)
  • Single-row h-scroll mode + 2-row stack mode (promotes at 3+ cats)
  • Right-edge fade gradient in scroll mode
  • Empty-result state for the feed area
BE deps
Extend get_prayer_wall RPC to accept { urgent: bool, categories: string[] }. OR within categories, AND on urgent. Talk to BE before you start.
Acceptance criteria
  • Tapping a category chip toggles selected; feed re-queries; active-count strip animates in (200 ms ease-out, height 0 → 36 pt)
  • Urgent chip toggles independently and stacks with category selections (AND logic)
  • Within-category logic is OR (≥2 cats selected returns posts tagged with any of them)
  • At 3 categories active, bar promotes to 2-row stack (active chips on top row, idle on second) — 200 ms ease-out
  • Clear chip appears the moment any chip is active; tap reverts everything to idle in one frame, no confirm
  • Active-count strip names the active filters in plain language + result count
  • Empty-result frame shows the centered serif sentence + Clear-filters pill
  • All sizing matches Surface 01 spec table — verify chip height 32 pt, padding 12 pt h, radius 16 pt, gap 8 pt
  • Reduced motion: drop 250 ms cross-fades to 1-frame swaps

Locked design decisions

v4 final · 24 May

  • Stand in the gap actionLives inside the detail sheet as a deliberate red button (heart-led, Connect-shape mirror). Card heart is display-only. Toggle semantics, persists per leader.
  • Card heart stateOutline = leader hasn't prayed; filled red = leader has prayed. Count next to it is the global total across all leaders.
  • After-state copy"You're standing in the gap" — present-continuous, gentle. Re-tap allowed but copy doesn't suggest the prayer is being un-done.
  • Celebrate (testimonies)One-tap on the card, no expand. Confetti popper icon with tap-burst (scale + rotate keyframes) + amber sparkle particles. Per-leader-once toggle.
  • Receive iconGlass less than half-full (vessel awaiting fullness). Pairs with the rising-incense bowl on Make: one rising, one waiting to be filled.
  • Make iconBowl of incense rising (Rev 5:8 — golden bowls full of incense, which are the prayers of the saints).
  • Scripture ruleNever truncates. Project-wide. Drop the type scale or break across more lines if it doesn't fit. No ellipsis, ever.
  • Card uniformityAll feed prayer cards line-clamp: 3 on body + › chevron in meta. My-open-prayers cards drop the chevron and use the overflow menu instead.
  • Connect availabilityDirect-message CTA enabled only when post is not anonymous and not underground-masked. Disabled state copy: "Anonymous · direct message unavailable."
  • Action-card weightHero action cards (Make / Receive) render at 30 pt Cormorant 500 with a 30 pt icon circle. They're doors, not chips.
  • Contact email[email protected]. (Switched from accounts@ earlier — this is the final.)
  • Categories v1Six chips: Healing · Protection · Unity · Provision · Praise · Manpower. Locked at v1.
  • "My" scope on Surface 04Church-wide, not user-wide. Every leader at a church sees every open request from any co-leader.
  • Soft-delete patternD-01 applies. Deleted requests flip is_active = false, never hard-deleted.
