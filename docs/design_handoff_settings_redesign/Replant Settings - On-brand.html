Brand moves

What's now Replant

  • Numbered eyebrow + serif title. Lifted directly from the brand kit (01 Mission & Foundation, 02 Logo & Mark…). Settings inherits the same composition.
  • Italic-serif live previews. Display-name radios now render their result in the brand's display face — Pastor Daniel / Pastor Daniel Mwangi — so the form is also the preview.
  • Mark watermark in the header. The rp circle sits opposite the back chevron, giving every deep screen a quiet mast.
  • Radial glow. Subtle ellipse behind the frame, matching the brand-kit cover gradient.

The Connect block

Lifted to mission

  • Old: a row at the bottom of About with hairline rules.
  • New: full-width treatment with the brand kit's Mission & Foundation structure — eyebrow with flanking hairlines, italic-serif address, DM Mono small-caps hint.
  • Same write contract — tap to copy + open mail. No CTA button. The address is the action.

Foundation

A quiet anchor at the foot

  • Replaces a bare Version 1.0.0 line with the brand-kit's foundational pattern: rp mark · italic scripture · KJV citation in DM Mono sky · version stamp underneath.
  • John 17:21 is the brand's named source of truth — it belongs at the bottom of the settings screen for the same reason it belongs at the bottom of every page on projectreplant.org.

Destructive footer

Solemn, not loud

  • Sign out is now serif — a name, not a button. Same weight register as page titles.
  • Deactivate account stays in DM Mono small-caps, lower opacity. It is meant to be quiet. The interaction weight lands on the step-up modal and consequence screen, not on the link.

Designer note

Why this feels Replant and the previous didn't.

The v1 Settings was structurally correct — every founder ruling consumed, every write contract right, every spacing rhythm honored. It read as a careful spec. It didn't read as Replant.

Three small structural moves close that gap. First, every section now leads with the same 01 — Title numbered eyebrow the brand kit uses for its own chapters. That single composition — DM Mono sky · Cormorant serif title · 28px hairline rule — is the brand's signature, and the screen earns its place in the system by using it.

Second, helper copy that previously sat in italicised sans is now rendered as italic-serif specimens in sky. The Display-name row no longer tells you what "Pastor Daniel" will look like; it shows you, in the typeface Replant will actually use to render that name on the wall. The form became a preview.

Third, the foot of the screen is no longer a version stamp. It is the brand's Mission & Foundation block in miniature: the rp mark, an italic line of John 17:21, the citation in DM Mono sky, and the version number beneath. On every other surface in the brand system, the foundation sits at the foot; Settings is no exception now.

A handful of smaller refinements support the change. The header gains a tiny rp watermark opposite the back chevron — a mast for deep screens. connect@projectreplant.org is no longer a row; it is its own composition, with flanking hairlines and an italic-serif address treated the way the brand kit treats its mission line. Sign out is now serif so it feels like a name rather than a button. The RAG radios carry small color dots, so the leader maps the option to what they see on the church map. Nothing functional changed. The screen simply remembered which brand it belongs to.
