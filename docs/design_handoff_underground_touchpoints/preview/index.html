What the queue sends back — pastoral notices on a watched device

Six in-app surfaces the verification queue produces for the underground leader. There is no inbox — every message arrives as a modal-on-launch plus a persistent Home-tab banner. The agent is always "the Replant team" — never "Admin," never a founder's name. The highest-leverage surface is the rejection notice: an underground leader may have risked exposure to register, so "rejected" lands like a door slammed. We say "could not be verified at this time." Dignity beats efficiency.

Father, for the leader who opens this app hoping for an answer — let what they read carry Your gentleness. For the one we cannot verify, guard their heart from shame; let "not yet" never sound like "not wanted." For the one whose church is now seen, and the one returning to hiding, keep them safe in Your hand. Make these few words pastoral, never corporate. In Jesus' name, Amen.

Deliverable 16 · Ruling #16

The team has a question — "there's no rush"

When an admin requests info, the leader sees a modal-on-launch on the Home tab, not a notification badge in some new inbox. The question itself sits in a scriptureItalic quote treatment — set apart, given weight, never buried. While the leader is in request-info state, the verified-gate phrase is suppressed: the team is waiting on them, not the other way around.

Deliverable 17 · Ruling #16

The reply — sent to the team, quietly confirmed

A single field, the question kept in view for context, a send action, and a calm post-send confirmation: "Your reply was sent to the team." No read receipts, no typing indicators, no urgency. The leader says what they can and closes the app in peace.

Deliverable 18 · Ruling #17 · highest-leverage surface

"Could not be verified at this time" — dignity over efficiency

The trauma-aware surface. Lead-in is always "After review, your registration could not be verified at this time." Then a reason-specific sentence, then the gentle close "You are welcome to re-apply when you're ready." with an appeal address. The safety_concern variant is different: no reason detail, no re-apply invitation — trauma-aware silence. We never make someone in danger feel they failed.

Deliverable 19 · Ruling #17

After the modal — a quiet banner that doesn't nag

Once the leader dismisses the outcome modal, the Home tab keeps a persistent banner so the message isn't lost: "Your registration could not be verified at this time." with a Read details → affordance that re-opens the full notice. It sits where the verification-pending banner used to — same chrome, generic enough that a screenshot reveals nothing underground-specific.

Deliverable 20 · Ruling #18

Before removal — blameless, time to appeal

Three days before a pending record is hard-deleted (day 23), a modal-on-launch in a blameless tone. The headline centers our wish to keep them — "We don't want to lose your registration" — and the body owns that the delay may be on our side ("Sometimes that's because we're still reviewing on our end"), with a path to appeal at accounts@projectreplant.org and a single "I understand" CTA. It never implies the leader dropped the ball.
