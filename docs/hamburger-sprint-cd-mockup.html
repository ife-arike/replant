02 · Invite to Replant — screen
Design Decision — Link Format Options
Two formats are shown for Founder selection. Recommendation: Option A (web URL) — universal, works as a link in bio, SMS, email, and any Android/iOS browser. Option B (deep link) only opens if the recipient already has the app installed, which defeats the purpose at the invite stage. Whichever format is chosen, the ?ref=CODE param registers the inviter in admin dashboard as "invited by". Code generation: unique 5-char alphanumeric per user, created on first visit to this screen and stored in users.referral_code (DB column to add, post-MVP or as part of invite ticket scope).

FAQ content note: Full answer copy for all 6 questions is below for CC implementation reference.
Q1 What is Replant? — Replant is a secure communication platform for verified Christian leaders globally. It connects pastors, ministers, and church workers across borders — with a particular focus on churches facing persecution or operating in restricted regions.
Q2 Who can join? — Replant is for verified church leaders — pastors, ministers, deacons, and recognised ministry workers. Each leader is verified by the Replant team before gaining full access to the network.
Q3 Data security — see mockup open state above.
Q4 What is a branch? — A branch is a group conversation within Replant, tied to a church or ministry context. Branch members can post messages, share prayer requests, and coordinate — separate from your direct messages.
Q5 Church verification? — Submit your church profile through the app. The Replant team reviews it and approves access — usually within 1–3 business days. You'll receive an in-app notification when your church is verified.
Q6 Contact? — Email the Replant team at accounts@projectreplant.org, or use the Settings → Contact form in the app.
